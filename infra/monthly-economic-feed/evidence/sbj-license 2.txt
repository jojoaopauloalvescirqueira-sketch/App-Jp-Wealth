Privacy Policy
1) Basic Policy
We collect information from users of this website within the scope necessary to ensure the smooth operation of the services provided on this website, as outlined below. Collected information will be handled appropriately within the scope of its intended purpose.
2) Scope of Information Collected and Purpose of Use
This website automatically collects information such as internet domain names, IP addresses, site search query information, and other data related to browsing this website. Cookies (information sent from the server to the user's browser and stored on the user's computer to identify the user) are limited to content intended to improve convenience and do not contain any personal information whatsoever. The collected information is used as a reference to ensure the smooth operation of the services provided on this website.
